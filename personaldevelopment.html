Reflection on The Richest Man in Babylon

Reading the chapters Seven Cures for a Lean Purse and Five Laws of Gold from The Richest Man in Babylon by George S. Clason made me think more about how I handle money. The first chapter explains that building wealth starts with simple habits like saving some of what you earn, controlling your spending, investing carefully and finding ways to earn more. As a student, I do not have a lot of money, but I can still start building these habits. The idea of saving at least one-tenth of what I earn stood out to me because I usually think I need to have more money before I can save. The chapter showed me that saving is more about building the habit than how much money I have.

Another lesson that stood out to me was controlling my spending. The book explains that our wants can keep growing until we spend all the money we have. I can relate to this because there are always things I could spend money on that I do not really need. I can use this lesson by making a budget and separating what I need from what I want. The chapter also teaches that money should be used to make more money and should be protected from loss. This means that when I start investing, I should understand what I am investing in instead of following something just because it promises quick or large returns.

The Five Laws of Gold also taught me important lessons. The laws explain that money grows when we save it and use it wisely. The story of Nomasir stood out to me because he lost his money by making bad decisions and trusting people without knowing enough. He later learned from his mistakes and used the five laws to rebuild his wealth. This showed me that having money is not enough. I also need to know how to manage and protect it. I should be careful with financial opportunities that I do not understand and avoid trying to get rich quickly.

I can use these lessons in my own life by being more careful with money and improving my ability to earn. As a Computing student, I know that the skills I build now can affect my future income. Improving my programming, problem-solving and other technical skills can help me get better opportunities in the future. I can also start saving some of the money I receive, keep track of my spending and learn more about investing. These may seem like small steps, but they can help me make better financial decisions later in life.

My biggest takeaway from the book is that having money and knowing how to manage money are two different things. Nomasir was given gold, but he lost it because he did not have enough knowledge or experience. He was later able to rebuild his wealth after learning how to manage money properly. This made me realize that I should not only focus on how much money I have. I should also focus on learning how to manage it. I may not have much money as a student right now, but I can start building good habits, improving my skills and preparing for my financial future.
